The mechanism

City LeadBuild

Three parts. Why the phone stays quiet. What we change. And the name for that work, so it is not “just SEO.”

  1. 01

    Why the phone stays quiet

    The Map Pack shows three businesses. If the profile, the citations, and the city page do not match the job, you are not one of the three. The competitor who did that work gets the call. A listing that exists is not the same thing as a listing that ranks for the job.

  2. 02

    What we change

    Look at the city and who is already in the Map Pack. Fill out the Google Business Profile for the jobs you sell. Make the name, phone, and address match. Write the service page for that city. Then watch calls, forms, and booked jobs.

  3. 03

    City Lead Build

    That is the name. It is the listing plus the page, for one city and one job. Not a pile of visits. The website and the ads, when you want them, ask for the same job.
